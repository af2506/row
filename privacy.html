Privacy Policy

AF Dashboard

Last updated August 29, 2026

AF Dashboard is a personal, single-user health & life-tracking app. This page explains what happens to your data when you connect the WHOOP integration on the Health page, and how the rest of the dashboard handles data in general.

Who this app is for

This is not a commercial or multi-tenant product. It's a private dashboard built and used by one person. If you're a WHOOP reviewer or another user evaluating this integration: the app is not distributed to the public and has no sign-up flow — access is limited to whoever deploys their own copy of this repository and connects their own WHOOP account.

What data the WHOOP integration accesses

When you tap "Connect WHOOP" and authorize the app, it requests read-only access to:

  • Recovery scores, HRV, resting heart rate, skin temperature, and blood oxygen
  • Sleep sessions and sleep stage breakdown (REM, deep, light, awake)
  • Daily strain and cycle data
  • Basic profile information

The app never requests write access and cannot modify or delete anything in your WHOOP account.

Where that data goes

It stays on your device. WHOOP data is fetched directly from WHOOP's API into your browser and stored only in that browser's local storage, to render the Health page. It is not written to any database, and it is not included in this dashboard's Supabase-based cloud sync (which is used only for goals, stack, water, and finance data). Nothing about your WHOOP data is shared with the developer of this app or any third party.

Authentication & tokens

WHOOP connect uses standard OAuth2. Your WHOOP Client Secret is kept server-side in a small set of serverless functions (/api/whoop-callback, /api/whoop-refresh) used only to exchange or refresh your access token — it never reaches the browser. Data reads go through /api/whoop-data, which forwards your access token to WHOOP's API without ever seeing the Client Secret. Your access and refresh tokens are stored in your browser's local storage and used only to make read requests to WHOOP's API on your behalf.

How to disconnect / delete your data

Tap "Disconnect" on the WHOOP card at any time to remove your tokens from this browser immediately. You can also revoke the app's access entirely from your WHOOP account settings at any time, which invalidates the tokens on WHOOP's side. Clearing your browser's site data for this dashboard removes everything stored locally, including WHOOP tokens and cached scores.

Other data in this dashboard

Outside of WHOOP, the dashboard's other pages (goals, supplement stack, water, finance, gym progress) store data in browser local storage and, if configured, sync it to a private Supabase project the deployer controls — used solely to keep the same person's data in sync across their own devices. None of that data is shared with any third party either.
